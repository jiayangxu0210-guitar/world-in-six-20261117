**The World in Six — 17 November 2026** is a single illustrated front page for six developments reported that Tuesday. The image is drawn by `draw.py` (Pillow + DejaVu fonts, no network, no randomness), and the Node 22 entry point `main.ts` copies the finished poster to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

To redraw the poster from scratch:

```sh
python3 draw.py
```

## Reporting notes

- **Kerch Strait:** Ukraine said its strike collapsed a span of the road bridge to occupied Crimea. Russia confirmed damage but did not specify its extent. [Associated Press via PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/amp/world/the-symbol-of-the-occupation-ukraine-destroys-span-of-kerch-bridge-linking-russia-with-crimea)
- **Americas:** PAHO announced that Bolivia, Mexico and the United States had each lost measles elimination status. [PAHO](https://www.paho.org/en/news/17-11-2026-paho-urges-countries-step-efforts-stop-measles-outbreaks-and-move-towards)
- **Sudan:** UN Human Rights reported that drone strikes accounted for more than 1,630 documented civilian deaths from January through October 2026. [OHCHR](https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2026/11/sudan-un-report-details-deadly-civilian-impact-surge-drone-strikes)
- **Manila:** The U.S. and Thai leaders signed a joint statement declaring negotiations substantially concluded. The trade agreement itself was not signed or in force that day. [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/11/joint-statement-on-an-agreement-between-the-united-states-of-america-and-the-kingdom-of-thailand-on-reciprocal-trade/)
- **Mongolia:** President Ukhnaagiin Khurelsukh announced he would step down amid corruption allegations. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/mongolian-president-announces-resignation-over-alleged-corruption-2026-11-17/)
- **Bay of Bengal:** India's weather agency issued warnings and forecast that Cyclone Akvan would cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coast on November 20. This was a forecast, not a landfall report. [India Meteorological Department](https://internal.imd.gov.in/press_release/20261117_pr_5497.pdf)
